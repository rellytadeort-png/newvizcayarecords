    Made in Nueva Vizcaya, for every Pinoy heart

    New Vizcaya Records started with one idea: songs that sound like the province. Rice fields at sunrise, the cold air on the way up to Santa Fe, the barkada around the table on a Friday night, and the hugot that hits on the bus ride home.

    Vizcaya Sound sings mostly in Tagalog, with a few tracks in English and Malay. For bookings, playlist features and collabs, send a message on Instagram or Facebook.

    Vizcaya Sound songs are made with the help of AI music tools and released independently by New Vizcaya Records.
